## 1. Muc dich
Thiet lap chuan ngon ngu thong nhat cho toan bo Vninvest theo nguyen tac:
- Nen tang la cong cu ho tro ra quyet dinh.
- Nen tang khong thay the quyet dinh dau tu cua nguoi dung.

Policy nay ap dung cho:
- UI/UX copy
- AI advisory output
- Realtime alerts
- Email/notification
- Landing page, fanpage, group, marketing content
- Terms, policy, disclaimer va tai lieu doi ngoai

## 2. Nguyen tac cot loi
1. Chinh xac va trung tinh
- Mo ta du lieu/su kien nhu no dang xay ra.
- Tranh ket luan tuyet doi.

2. Xac suat thay vi khang dinh
- Dung cac cum: "nghieng ve", "kha nang", "muc tin cay", "can xac nhan them".

3. Co canh bao rui ro
- Moi de xuat hanh dong can co doan nhac rui ro va gioi han du lieu.

4. Ton trong quyen tu quyet
- Ngon ngu phai dat nguoi dung o vai tro ra quyet dinh cuoi cung.

5. Nhat quan da kenh
- Cung mot thong diep cot loi tren app, website, social, email.

## 3. Tu vung duoc phep va khong duoc phep
### 3.1 Tu/cum nen dung
- "ho tro ra quyet dinh"
- "goi y tham khao"
- "tin hieu"
- "kich ban"
- "muc tin cay"
- "can kiem chung"
- "quan tri rui ro"

### 3.2 Tu/cum can tranh
- "chac chan"
- "bao dam loi nhuan"
- "lenh phai mua/ban"
- "an chac"
- "khong the lo"
- "tat tay" / "all-in"

## 4. Mau cau chuan theo boi canh
### 4.1 AI advisory
Mau:
- "Tin hieu hien tai nghieng ve kich ban [X] voi muc tin cay [thap/trung binh/cao]. Day la thong tin tham khao, vui long ket hop khau vi rui ro va ke hoach von."

### 4.2 Realtime alert
Mau:
- "Phat hien bien dong [gia/vol/dong tien] vuot nguong. Vui long xac nhan them voi bo chi bao khac truoc khi ra quyet dinh."

### 4.3 CTA nang cap Pro
Mau:
- "Mo khoa cong cu phan tich nang cao de ho tro ra quyet dinh nhanh hon va co he thong hon."

### 4.4 Disclaimer ngan trong app
Mau:
- "Nen tang cung cap cong cu va thong tin ho tro ra quyet dinh, khong phai khuyen nghi dau tu ca nhan hoa."

## 5. Khung trinh bay 4 lop cho moi thong diep phan tich
Moi thong diep phan tich quan trong nen co 4 lop:
1. Du lieu quan sat duoc
- Vi du: "Khoi luong tang 2.1x so voi trung binh 20 phien."

2. Dien giai tin hieu
- Vi du: "Dong tien ngan han co dau hieu cai thien."

3. Rui ro/gioi han
- Vi du: "Gia van duoi nguong khang cu cu, kha nang false breakout con ton tai."

4. Hanh dong tham khao
- Vi du: "Co the uu tien quan sat va vao lenh tung phan khi co xac nhan."

## 6. Quy chuan cho free vs paid communication
1. Ban Free
- Nhan manh trai nghiem va gia tri nen tang.
- Khong gay so hai de ep nang cap.

2. Ban Paid (annual)
- Nhan manh gia tri tang them: toc do, do sau phan tich, quota, workflow.
- Khong dung ngon ngu "neu khong nang cap se mat co hoi chac chan".

## 7. Checklist review bat buoc truoc khi publish
Moi noi dung ra ben ngoai phai pass tat ca cau hoi sau:
1. Co su dung tu ngu tuyet doi/hua hen loi nhuan khong?
2. Co neu ro muc tin cay hoac do khong chac chan khong?
3. Co nhac den rui ro/gioi han du lieu khong?
4. Co giu dung vai tro "ho tro" thay vi "thay the" khong?
5. Co nhat quan voi disclaimer chuan cua du an khong?

## 8. Governance va van hanh
1. Owner
- Product + Compliance dong phe duyet policy va cap nhat dinh ky.

2. Chu ky cap nhat
- Moi quy review 1 lan
- Cap nhat dot xuat khi co yeu cau phap ly moi

3. Xu ly vi pham
- Noi dung vi pham policy phai go/chinh sua truoc khi tiep tuc phat hanh.

## 9. Ban tom tat 1 cau de dong bo toan du an
"Vninvest la nen tang cong cu ho tro ra quyet dinh dau tu dua tren du lieu; quyet dinh cuoi cung thuoc ve nguoi dung."
