# Duong nang cap tu Community len SaaS hoac VIP

## Community -> SaaS

1. Nguoi dung bat dau voi bundle Community/OpenCore.
2. Khi can tenant admin, quota cao hon hoac commercial controls, ho chuyen sang SaaS.
3. Du lieu va contract phai di qua `packages/core`, khong quay ve frontend clone rieng.

## Community -> VIP

1. Nguoi dung can local backend va local control plane.
2. VIP giu cung frontend source voi web, nhung dong goi thanh lane local.
3. OpenCore chi dong vai tro funnel dan nguoi dung vao lane phu hop.

## Nguyen tac chung

- Khong tao mot frontend source thu hai cho OpenCore.
- Khong de OpenCore tru thanh backend truth rieng.
- Moi upgrade phai giu duong di ro rang, co contract va evidence voi core.
