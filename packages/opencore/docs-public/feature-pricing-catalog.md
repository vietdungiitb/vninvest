# Danh muc tinh nang va dinh vi gia

## Muc dich

Tai lieu nay chi mo ta public-safe feature positioning, khong cong bo secret hay internal policy.

## Bang tom tat

| Lane | Tinh nang chinh | Dinh vi gia |
|---|---|---|
| Community / OpenCore | Chart, watchlist, danh muc con ban, public onboarding docs, co duong upgrade ro rang | Mien phi / self-host co ban |
| SaaS | Tenant admin, commercial controls, data-plane contracts, support chinh thuc | Bao gia theo tenant, SLA va dung luong |
| VIP | Local backend, local admin, private AI tooling, on-prem deployment | Bao gia theo trien khai, so luong nguoi dung va connector |

## Luu y

- OpenCore khong so huu entitlement truth.
- Gia chinh thuc cho tenant lon hoac VIP co the duoc cung cap qua kenh ban hang.
- Public docs chi can du thong tin de nguoi dung hieu duoc duong nang cap.
